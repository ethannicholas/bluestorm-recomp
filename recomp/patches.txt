# The spray (docs/dev/performance.md, fifth pass): fn_8002F478 spawns each rider's spray
# particles at the hull once a frame, and the particles then live a count of frames. At
# 60 that is twice the particles a second, and the water near the field washes white; at
# 60 the spawner runs on every other frame.
# The spawner also runs the particles' update (fn_8002FABC at 0x8002F8CC), which sets the
# per-rider draw stride at 0x804EB080 (1, 2 or -1: never 0 once set); the drawer
# fn_8002F9EC runs every frame and steps through the particles by that stride, so a frame
# that draws after a course reset cleared the table and before the spawner has run again
# loops forever (the "WR_FPS=60 from boot hangs at the course load", and the headset's
# black screen at a load after a toggle to 60, 2026-10-11). The skip is taken only while
# the table still holds what the spawner last left there (wr_spray_gate / wr_spray_ran,
# runtime/pace.cpp, which has why the two earlier tests of the entries were wrong).
